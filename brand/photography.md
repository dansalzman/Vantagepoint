# Photography

The subject of every image is a competent employee at work. Some photos show a visible disability and some do not, which matches the people Vantage Point serves: many disabilities are not apparent.

## Direction

Do

- Mid-task, hands on the work, engaged with a colleague or customer.
- Eye level, natural light, real workplaces.
- A mix of sectors that matches real placements: offices, retail, food service, healthcare support, warehouse and logistics, skilled trades.
- Navy or sky somewhere in the frame through clothing or setting, so photos sit with the palette without a filter.
- Mix the two groups below on any page. A page of only wheelchair users in offices misrepresents the client base.

Avoid

- Inspiration framing: the lone figure, the helper leaning in, the applauding team.
- Cropping to a wheelchair, cane or hearing aid as the focal point.
- Staged handshakes and people smiling at nothing.
- A coach doing the task while the client watches.
- Color overlays or heavy grading that flatten skin tones.

## Formats

| Use | Ratio | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero, web and title slide | 16:9 or 3:2 | Full bleed or `radius-lg` corners. Leave room for copy on a navy panel beside it, not text over the photo. |
| Card and content slide | 3:2 or 4:5 | `radius-lg` corners. |
| Portrait and divider slide | 1:1 | Circle crop with a 4 px `brand-sky` ring (RingPhoto). |

## Licensing

All 25 photos are from Pexels under the Pexels license: free for commercial use, cropping and editing allowed, credit not required. The license does not allow showing identifiable people in a bad light or implying that they endorse an organization, so do not caption a stock model as a Vantage Point client or attach a quote to them. The Pexels photo ID for each file is listed in the Photography asset group.

These are stock models. Whether each person has the disability shown is not known. Replace the set with photographs of real clients, with signed releases, as soon as a shoot is possible.
